# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository owner’s private reporting channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove, warn, or otherwise restrict participants who violate this Code. This Code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
